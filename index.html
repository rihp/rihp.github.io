Philosophy

The most interesting AI problems are rarely the hardest ones technically. They're the ones where solving them actually changes someone's day. That bias toward human impact shapes every architecture decision I make and every sprint I run.

Most teams split into people who understand the technology and people who understand the business. I've spent years in both. That combination shows up in how I work: shipping agents tied to measurable outcomes, leading engineering teams with product clarity, and knowing when a prototype needs to become a system versus when it needs to be scrapped.
